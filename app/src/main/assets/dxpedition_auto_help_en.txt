DXpedition Hound auto mode

When enabled:
- FT8 DXpedition compound messages can drive the automatic QSO program.
- After Fox sends your signal report, the app advances to the Hound R+rpt message.
- After Fox sends RR73, the app closes the QSO and returns to normal auto flow.

When disabled:
- DXpedition messages are still decoded and shown in the UI.
- Free text mode and experimental modem modes are unchanged.
- You can still transmit manually, but the automatic program will not sequence DXpedition replies.

This switch only affects FT8 DXpedition Hound behavior. Standard FT8 and FT4 automatic QSOs are unchanged.
